Sigelith - naming announcement
==============================

Date:     27 September 2026 (UTC)
Operator: Adam Koch, Hagen, Germany (legal notice: https://beattime.live/de/impressum/)


1. The name

Sigelith is the name of the proof-of-existence infrastructure first
published as "BeatTime proof" at https://beattime.live/ : the public
append-only log of SHA-256 fingerprints, its signed checkpoints, the
certificates, the verification API, the time capsules, the key server
for sealed capsules, the SDKs and the desktop client.

BeatTime remains the name of the time notation (.beat) and of the clock
apps.

3. What does not change

The log, its entries, checkpoints, anchors and signing keys stay exactly
as they are. The format identifiers inside signed data
(beattime-proof-v1, beattime-entry-v1, beattime-checkpoint-v1) keep their
names, because they are part of what is signed. sigelith.org and
beattime.live are served by one system, with one log and one key, and
every proof issued under the name BeatTime keeps verifying at both.

Naming history: https://sigelith.org/spec/#naming


4. This file

Bitcoin. The name Sigelith is attested by the system it denotes.
